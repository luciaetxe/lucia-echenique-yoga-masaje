About me

Trained in traditional massage in Thailand, I offer, from presence and listening, a few moments to stop, let go and come back to the body — here and now.

I've taken many paths since, at 19, I decided I wanted to go to the heart of the Amazon with no return ticket. I may come across as someone with diverse, scattered interests, but they all share one thing: the study of what is human, and of experience. I'm currently studying psychology, a science I draw on to understand us better.

The massage

Thai Yoga Massage is a contemporary approach to traditional Thai massage that brings back the spiritual dimension of this ancient tradition. It takes place on a futon, in comfortable clothes and without oils.

Given with focus and presence, it combines rhythmic pressure and passive stretches to work the whole body, release and tone the tissues, and restore the nervous system.
